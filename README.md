Star Conquest is a fast-paced, top-down 2D space strategy and dogfighting game inspired by classics like Spacewar! and Solar Conquest.
Core Gameplay & Objectives
Interplanetary Conquest: Pilot a starfighter across multi-star solar systems, claiming unclaimed worlds and bombarding enemy fortifications.
Planetary Colonization: Landing on an unowned or cleared planet claims it for your faction. Your territory automatically dispatches massive interstellar Colony Ships that build surface installations (military bases, shield generators, and orbital defense turrets).
Surface & Orbital Defense: Armed installations and space stations defend your planets, firing defensive laser salvos at approaching hostile ships.
Combat, Upgrades & Features
Tactical Dogfighting: Fast-paced momentum physics, inertia drifting, thrusters, and hyper-boost.
Weapon Buffs & Upgrades: Respawning or touching down on colonized worlds grants chances for 25 HP Energy Shields, 
 Double Damage, and Smart Homing Missiles that lock onto and destroy enemy ships, planetary installations, space stations, and colony ships.
Unique AI Archetypes: Rival factions fight with distinct behaviors—including aggressive Berserkers who hold grudges, systematic Defenders who secure home star systems before expanding, and territorial Expansionists who hunt colony arks and rapidly claim new worlds.
Live Minimap & HUD: Real-time galaxy radar tracking star systems, planet ownership, ship movements, and traveling colony ships.
